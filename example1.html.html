// Clarity & Fairness

Clear Membership Terms

No predatory lock-ins. We pride ourselves on transparent neighborhood policies designed for mutual respect and safety.

Individual & Non-Transferable

Memberships are designated strictly for one individual and cannot be sold, transferred, or shared. Every member has their own verified photo profile.

Mandatory digital credential

30 Days’ Notice Cancellation

Month-to-month memberships offer genuine flexibility with a straightforward 30 days’ written notice required prior to billing cycle termination.

Simple cancellation process

$10/Mo Membership Freeze

Traveling or busy? Freeze your membership for only $10 per month, for up to 3 months per calendar year. Medical freezes are gladly reviewed with documentation.

Medical exemptions honored

Age 16+ Requirement

All members must be at least 16 years of age. Members aged 16–17 must have a parent or legal guardian cosign the membership agreement on file.

Guardian verification required

Billing & Account Standing

Automated recurring dues keep operations seamless. Any missed payments may temporarily pause turnstile access until the account balance is rectified.

Contact desk for payment updates

Guest Check-In Policy

Guests are welcome during staffed hours. All visitors must check in at the desk, present valid government photo ID, execute our waiver, and observe gym rules.

Waiver required upon arrival
